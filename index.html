Who we are

We’re a student-led FTC team combining mechanical design, software, strategy, and outreach. Members learn CAD, Java/Kotlin (FTC SDK), electronics, and how to present our work to judges and the community.

Design & build

From intake to auton, we iterate fast with documented design decisions and a shared parts philosophy.

Compete with integrity

Scouting, match strategy, and Gracious Professionalism® on and off the field.

Grow the pipeline

Workshops, demos, and mentorship so the next generation sees STEM as theirs to shape.
